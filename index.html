Realtime Roads

Realtime Roads is a research project led by Morgan Skillicorn at Abertay University, originating from his MSc thesis. The project explores how Unreal Engine 5.7 and procedurally generated environments can dramatically improve the flexibility and reliability of on‑set content. By creating fully controllable, choreographed virtual road environments, Realtime Roads overcomes the limitations of traditionally captured 2D plates. Integrated tracking systems within the Unreal scene ensure accurate perspective and parallax, resulting in more convincing, dynamic, and production‑ready shots for modern virtual filmmaking.
